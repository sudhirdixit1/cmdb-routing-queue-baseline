# Declaration of competing interests

The author declares that he has no known competing financial interests or
personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work
reported in this paper.

No funding was received for this work. The author holds no consulting
relationship, equity stake, employment or advisory position with any vendor
of configuration-management, IT service management or AIOps software, and no
relationship with either of the organisations whose event logs are analysed
(Rabobank Group ICT and Volvo IT). Both logs are public benchmark datasets
released for the Business Process Intelligence Challenge; neither
organisation was contacted, involved in, or informed of this study, and
neither has reviewed it.
