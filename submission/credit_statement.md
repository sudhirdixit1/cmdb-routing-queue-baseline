## Generative-AI use, in short

The title page carries the declaration Elsevier requires; this is the same
disclosure in short. A large-language-model assistant (Anthropic Claude) was
used in three ways: to draft and edit prose; to write the analysis and
verification code; and to conduct internal adversarial reviews of each built
manuscript against the repository, which are the author's own reviews made
with a tool and not reports from any journal's referees. No generative model
produced, imputed, augmented or selected any datum, any result or any citation
behind a claim of this paper. The one place a machine-assisted judgement
enters a study is the literature pilot's adjudication (Supplement S23), which
is declared where it is described, and no claim in the paper rests on it.
Every session's model identifier and dates are in `AI-USE.md` in the repository; the archived release (v28.0) was cut before the sessions that followed it.
