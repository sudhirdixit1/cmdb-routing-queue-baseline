\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:lim}

Organised as threats rather than as a list. No result is computed here:
every number this section quotes is the same macro, from the same result
file, as the section that establishes it, so the two cannot disagree --- but
many are quoted twice, and a reader who wants the argument rather than the
summary should read them where they are established.

\paragraph{Construct --- this is forecast performance, not value}
Nothing here measures what a register costs to acquire or maintain, whether
acting on a forecast changes an outcome, or what a data programme is worth;
that includes the layer comparison of Supplement~\ref{app:programmeread}. The corpus is a
family of related questions rather than replications --- the two registered
targets label the same case identically on a median \targetAgreement\ of
cases, and the registers run from a configuration item to a legal article ---
so every corpus statistic is about \emph{sensitivity}, every one is reported
on the \nItsmPairs\ ITSM pairs as well (Table~\ref{tab:family}), and
Table~\ref{tab:construct} names each pair's own construct threat. The
registered handover target on the case study's log is close to degenerate at
prevalence \prevAdmittedMax, carries \nCellsBpicFourteenHandover\ of
\nDeclaredAdmissibleScalar\ admissible cells, and is where
Section~\ref{sec:cohort} shows the register's sign turns; whether a generic
handover rule is a meaningful target on ITSM logs and whether the prevalence
window should close below \prevHi\ are open, and the rule was not narrowed
after seeing which pair it admits. The quality mechanisms are applied to both
halves with parameters estimated from training alone
(Section~\ref{sec:quality}), so they are the operational counterfactual and
not a train--test mismatch, but they remain constructed, and validating them
against a genuinely incomplete register is the obvious next study.

\paragraph{Internal --- what the band's coverage does and does not establish}
Section~\ref{sec:simband} measures family-wise coverage against a known
answer over matched families whose bootstrap is \emph{ideal}, so every
coverage there is an upper bound on what the procedure attains. On the family
matched to the design this paper reports the empirical critical value covers
\covEmpHeavyWholeAtDesign\ under the corpus's tails and
\covEmpNonzeroWholeAtDesign\ under a non-zero truth, against a nominal
$95\%$, at a Monte Carlo standard error of about
\covEmpHeavyWholeAtDesignSE; the multiplier approximation covers
\covMultHeavyWholeAtDesign\ and \covMultNonzeroWholeAtDesign, and no draw
budget closes that gap. So the region labels and $\rho$ rest on a critical
value measured at its level, with three qualifications that are limits
rather than results; Supplement~\ref{app:threats} states them, with the
mechanisms this evidence retires and the further assumptions it names, one
of them measured.

\paragraph{Conclusion --- two claims a specification surface invites and
this evidence does not support}
Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} withdraws both readings a pooled surface invites,
together with the sign-disagreement rate as a headline. Three qualifications
of another kind are limits rather than results: both decompositions are reported under all
three declared measures because there is no neutral one (the higher-order
share moves by \measureSpreadPct\ across them), the within-instrument
decomposition being primary because the decomposition inherits the metric's
scale (Proposition~\ref{prop:scale}); region labels are relative to an
inference family of a median \inferenceShareAdmissibleMedianPct\ of a pair's
admissible cells, under a minimum resolved share that withdraws the direction
on \nLabelsLostToMinShare\ of \nDirectionalLabels\ pairs, and stay
conditional on the quality axis being averaged over; and specification regret
reports a null and is a robustness check rather than a contribution.

\paragraph{External --- the design space is declared, not complete}
$\mathcal{S}$ has \nAxes\ axes in Definition~\ref{def:space} and the per-pair
factorial varies \nPerPairAxes\ of them (Table~\ref{tab:roles}); the decision
time, the operating point and the cohort vary on the case study's log alone,
and the target indexes surfaces rather than varying within one. It could have
more --- tuning protocol, missing-data treatment, target horizon, label-noise
model --- so the honest claim is ``the increment moves this much over
\emph{this} space''. The structural omission is that prefix length, prefix
bucketing and sequence encoding are not axes here and cannot be, because this
study forecasts at one origin from creation-time attributes, and
those are the axes predictive process monitoring varies most; we therefore
claim no result about that literature's own benchmark. A prefix axis is
nonetheless run on one log (Supplement~\ref{app:prefix}): holding the
register and the estate fixed and moving only the prefix, the increment falls
from \VPrefixZero\ AUC at case creation to \VPrefixTwo\ two events later and
\VPrefixEight\ at the longest prefix run, and is undetermined in sign at \nPrefixUnresolved\ of \nPrefixLevels\ prefixes,
so case creation --- the point every other result here occupies --- is the
most favourable prefix for a creation-time register, and two studies
reporting from the two ends of that axis would differ by a factor of
\prefixDropFactor\ without either being wrong. One log, one register and no
sequence encoder is a pilot, and the omission stands.

\paragraph{External --- three narrower limits, named}
The inference surface is smaller than the declared one and always will be,
because a draw refits every arm of every cell (Section~\ref{sec:threesurfaces}),
so every region label is a claim about a fraction of the space the point
estimates cover, on which \emph{uniformly beneficial} is easier to earn. The
pipeline axis is crossed on the \nItsmCrossed\ ITSM pairs and not elsewhere,
and what the crossing found is not a clean replication
(Section~\ref{sec:axes2}), so the case study's ordering is the common case
and not a corpus fact. The case study is one bank's estate on one public log,
with the availability question at its centre settled by probability rather
than by a timestamped field-value history; Supplement~\ref{app:programmeread} names
the three changes that would move which answer. And reporting practice is not
measured: the literature pilot that attempted it is in the archive as a
hypothesis.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

What a predictor adds to a forecast is a functional of design choices, not a
number. This paper made that operational: a declared
design space, a surface over it, an exact decomposition of the surface's
variance that separates the choices an analyst makes from the resampling the
design also contains, simultaneous bands whose coverage is measured rather
than assumed, and region labels that say where a sign is determined and
withhold a direction where too little of the surface resolves to support
one.

\textbf{On every pair, at least one admissible specification makes the
register worth less than nothing.} That is a statement about point estimates
and needs no band. It is also nearly guaranteed by a space that includes
three-quarters-deleted registers, so the version worth carrying is the
restricted one: \textbf{on \nSpreadExceedsRealistic\ of \nPairs\ pairs the
baseline alone --- among the baselines an analyst would actually build ---
moves the increment further than the increment itself}. Four further results
are worth carrying away.

\textbf{Most of a pooled specification surface is resampling, and net of it
different pairs turn on different analyst choices.} The components involving
the split carry a median \shareResamplingStratumPct\ of the variance. Net of them the largest first-order index at the median pair is
\foldLargestFirstOrderPct\ against a higher-order share of
\foldInteractionTotalPct: the interactions carry more than the largest
first-order index within an instrument on
\nFoldInteractionExceeds\ of \nPairs\ pairs, against
\nPooledInteractionExceeds\ before the resampling stratum is removed, so
\emph{most of the variance belongs to no single axis} is not a claim this
evidence supports (Section~\ref{sec:axes2}). What it does support is that
which axis leads is a property of the pair --- the baseline rung on
\nFoldLeadPairsRung\ pairs, the register-quality condition on
\nFoldLeadPairsQuality\ and the pipeline on \nFoldLeadPairsPipeline. An
analyst therefore cannot learn from somebody else's paper which of their own
choices their answer turns on --- but neither should they read a pooled
specification curve as evidence that everything matters.

\textbf{A one-number report is usually right about the sign and usually silent
about the size.} Once a sign has to be determined by the data \emph{and} worth
acting on at \mpidAuc\ AUC, the number of the
\nCellsFullyRestricted\ \emph{AUC} cells surviving all three restrictions
that contradict the conventional report is
\nDisagreeFullyRestricted{} --- the MPID is declared in AUC and
Remark~\ref{prop:nonid} forbids carrying it further. \textbf{So the
sign-disagreement rate is withdrawn as a headline}
(Section~\ref{sec:results-regret}): a sign disagreement is not a
misstatement unless the sizes are worth acting on, and under the three
restrictions this paper's own argument implies, they are not.
What the conventional report does not say is that the cells the band
\emph{resolves} lie a median \madResolvedMedian\ AUC away from the one it
stands on, and that on
\nRefUnresolvedCell\ of \nPairs\ pairs the data do not determine the sign of
its own cell at all.

\textbf{The forecast origin is an argument of the estimand, not a preliminary.}
It fixes which cases are in scope, what is known about them, and which fields
are admissible. On the case study, moving the forecast from first touch to
incident creation moves the increment by \deltaCohortAtTau\ AUC through the
population, by a further \deltaSnapshotAtTau\ through the information once the
population is held fixed, and by \deltaPopulationResidual\ more through the
incidents that have no interaction record at all --- before any baseline changes; and the same
log's answer changes sign when the target changes, with \shareTargetPct\ of
the difference on the target and \shareCohortPct\ on the cohort. A desk that
adopted this register on its \emph{first-touch} number and deployed it at
incident creation would be promised \promisedPerThousand\ true positives per
thousand cases and receive \deliveredPerThousand.

\textbf{And a threshold is not always a cost ratio.} On \nModelsDcaExcluded\
of \nModelsDca\ fitted models the calibration slope falls outside
\calSlopeWindow, so the threshold at which a decision curve is read cannot be
interpreted as the cost ratio the decision-analytic argument requires
(Section~\ref{sec:calibfirst}).

The practical recommendation is narrow and cheap once the surface exists:
report the surface, the decomposition with the resampling stratum separated,
the region and $\rho$ with the triple they rest on; state the forecast origin
and the target as parts of the estimand; declare a minimal practically
important difference before reporting any disagreement rate; make the absolute
increments primary; and use a ratio last, with a Fieller set, and only where
its denominator is resolvably positive.
