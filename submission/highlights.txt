HIGHLIGHTS

Elsevier allows three to five bullets, each at most 85 characters including
spaces; Springer's guidance for Empirical Software Engineering is the same
shape. These are the five, with their lengths, so a future edit can check the
limit without re-counting by hand.

**Do not count these by hand.**  `python scripts/check_highlights.py` derives
every length below and fails if one is wrong or over the limit; `--fix`
rewrites them.  The file has now shipped twice with hand-counted lengths that
were wrong -- round sixteen's five were wrong by 3 to 11 characters, and round
eighteen's rewrite got four of nine wrong on the first attempt, in a file whose
own preamble warned against exactly that.  A count a human writes is a claim
like any other and belongs in a script.

The fourth of these names the round's most transferable finding -- a defect

name a methodological point over ones that name a magnitude.

-- what changed in round eighteen, and why --

The second bullet is new and it replaces the instrument-range bullet. The
reason is that the instrument range is a magnitude and the audit result is a
*finding about the field*, which is what a reader who stops at the highlights
most needs. It is also the sharpest single sentence the paper can offer: 20
papers read, and not one of them reports what a feature is worth across a
range of operating points.

The fifth bullet's count moved from eleven to twelve. The twelfth was found
by an independent re-implementation of our own estimator, on its first run.

-- a note on the fourth bullet --

"Falsifies our generality claim" is deliberate and is not hedged. A referee
who reads only the highlights should learn from them that the corpus result
is negative, because that is what a reader who stops there most needs to
know. Section 12 states the verdict precisely: the claim registered in
PROTOCOL.md section 8 is falsified by its own criterion, and on 10 of the 19
log-target pairs the entity is worth nothing to the intake baseline, so the
corpus is uninformative about a narrower claim rather than refuting one.

-- a note on the second bullet, because it is the one a referee will test --

"Of 20 audited papers" is the honest denominator and it is not the 600 the
audit enumerated. The funnel is 600 sampled, 369 full texts retrieved, 54
included by a mechanical screen, 30 read, 20 confirmed in scope by that read.
The bullet says 20 because 20 is the number the claim rests on.
