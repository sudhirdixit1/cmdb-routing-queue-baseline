# Declaration of competing interests

The author declares the following employment, which may be considered a
potential competing interest: the author is employed by Cognizant as a
Solutions Architect. This work was done independently of that employment,
without its support or data, and Cognizant had no role in it.

No funding was received for this work. Beyond the employment above, the
author holds no consulting relationship, equity stake, employment or advisory
position with any vendor of configuration management, IT service management
or process mining software, and no relationship with any of the organisations
whose event logs are analysed. Every log is a public dataset released for research, several
of them for the Business Process Intelligence Challenge; no organisation was
contacted, involved in, or informed of this study, and none has reviewed it.

declarations tool and attached as the `.docx` it produces, without
conversion: fill that tool from this text and attach its `.docx`, not this
