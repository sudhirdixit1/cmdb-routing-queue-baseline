
\section*{Data availability}

All data are public. Event logs are obtained by DOI: BPI Challenge 2014
(\doiBPICfourteen), BPI Challenge 2013 incidents (\doiBPICthirteen), and the
further public logs whose DOIs the archive's fetch scripts carry and whose
SHA-256 checksums are listed in \checksumfile\ --- \nCorpusDownloaded\ files are
downloaded and checksummed in all, one log shipping as three, and the
registered role-assignment rules of Section~\ref{sec:design} admit \nLogs\
logs, yielding the \nPairs\
log--target pairs analysed here. The counts are different quantities and
Table~\ref{tab:corpus} accounts for every downloaded log that is not among the
admitted pairs, in one of two ways: with the registered exclusion code the
rules gave it, or with the code \textsf{HELD\_OUT}, which marks the log downloaded as a
held-out log for a registered prediction outside this paper and never
offered to the admission rules (\nHeldOutLogs\ of the checksummed files); the
second log downloaded for it was offered to the rules and is listed under
the codes that excluded it. The literature pilot's frame, sample,
codes and adjudications are in the archive; the retrieved full texts and
the evidence dossiers extracted from them are other publishers' copyrighted
material and are not redistributed, but \pilotfetch\ re-retrieves the texts
from the DOIs listed in \pilotsamplefile, and the same script's dossier
stage rebuilds the dossiers. Every analysis result file is in the archive named in the code-availability
statement, byte for byte (the harness's own bookkeeping files there post-date
it); the code, the figures and the manuscript source at the repository's
head post-date the archive, and where an archived generator would print a
summary differently the correction register (Supplement~\ref{app:corrections})
says so.

\section*{Code availability}

Code, derived results, the registered protocols, the amendment log and the
verification harness are archived under a persistent identifier and
developed in a public repository; both are given on the title page and are
omitted here for double-blind reviewing. The archive contains a
\texttt{Dockerfile} and a \texttt{requirements.lock} pinning every
transitive dependency, and \reproduceguide\ gives the exact commands
and the expected runtime.
