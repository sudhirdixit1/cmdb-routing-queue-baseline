\section*{Code availability}

Code, derived results, the registered protocols, the amendment log and the
verification harness are archived at \zenodoDOI\ (release \releaseTag) and
developed at \url{https://github.com/sudhirdixit1/cmdb-routing-queue-baseline}.
The archived release holds every derived result byte for byte; the
manuscript source, the figures and the generators as submitted are at the
repository's head, which post-dates it, and the three summaries the archived
generators would print differently are corrections the supplement's register
records.
The archive contains a \texttt{Dockerfile} and a \texttt{requirements.lock}
pinning every transitive dependency; \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} gives the exact
commands and the expected runtime. \textbf{Bit-exact reproduction is claimed
inside that container --- \imageDigest\ --- and not outside it.} On a different processor architecture, cells whose design carries no
high-cardinality register agree to floating-point tolerance, while cells that
carry one can differ materially in every metric --- the register ties test
rows to equal predictions, and a difference in the last bit unties them, so a
rank-based metric steps. The divergence tracks the register's cardinality and
not the learner: it is present in both model families and absent from the
low-cardinality rungs of each. \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} measures it for both
families and states a tolerance per condition, and
\texttt{scripts/check\_reproduction.py} re-runs part of the surface and fails
if one is exceeded. A reader checking a \emph{digit} therefore needs the
container; a reader checking a \emph{conclusion} does not.

\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process}

During the preparation of this work the author used Anthropic Claude
(\aiModelIdPrev, then \aiModelId\ again; \aiModelDate) in order
to draft and edit prose, to write analysis and verification code, and to
conduct internal adversarial reviews of the manuscript against the
repository. After using this tool the author reviewed and edited the content
as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the publication.

\noindent \textbf{This is a use beyond the language-and-readability editing an
author may make without comment, which is why it is declared}, and its three
parts are separated here because the policy this section answers is about the
first: the prose was drafted and edited with the tool; the analysis and
verification code was written with it, which is a use in the research process
and is disclosed rather than assumed to be covered; and the manuscript was
read by the tool in the role of a reviewer between revisions, with every
objection and its disposition recorded in \texttt{REFEREE-LOG.md} in the
repository, which is why that log is not a record of the journal's own review.
The tool is named with its version and the dates it was used, because a
disclosure that names a product and not a version is not reproducible; every
session's model identifier and date are in \texttt{AI-USE.md} in the
repository, including the sessions whose identifier was not recorded at the
time; the archived release was cut before the sessions that followed it, and
the repository carries the full register.
\textbf{No generative model produced,
imputed, augmented or selected any datum, any result or any citation behind a
claim of this paper}: every result is produced by public code that
\texttt{REPRODUCE.md} re-runs by one command, and the reference list was
checked against the published records. The one place where a machine-assisted
judgement enters a study is the literature pilot of
Supplement~\ref{app:pilot}, which is declared where it is described, and no
claim in this paper rests on it.
