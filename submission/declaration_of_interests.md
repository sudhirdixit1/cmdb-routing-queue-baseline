# Declaration of competing interests

The author declares that they have no known competing financial interests or
personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work
reported in this paper.

No funding was received for this work. The author holds no consulting
relationship, equity stake, employment or advisory position with any vendor
of configuration management, IT service management or process mining
software, and no relationship with any of the organisations whose event logs
are analysed. Every log is a public dataset released for research, several
of them for the Business Process Intelligence Challenge; no organisation was
contacted, involved in, or informed of this study, and none has reviewed it.
