\begin{abstract}
Forecast evaluation asks whether a predictor improves out-of-sample
probability forecasts over a nested benchmark, answering with one score
difference dependent on unstated choices: the benchmark, model class,
split, forecast origin, predictor completeness and scoring rule. We treat
the improvement as a surface over declared choices, with three
reporting objects: an exact variance decomposition separating
forecaster choices from split-to-split variation; simultaneous bootstrap
bands, their family-wise coverage measured by simulation; and resolution
regions, where its sign is determined. Across \nPairs\ binary forecasting tasks from
\nLogs\ public event logs, mostly IT-service and
administrative, the evaluation split and its interactions carry a median
\shareResamplingStratumPct\ of the variance, and resolved specifications (\nPairsMadResolved\ tasks) lie a
median \madResolvedMedian\ in area under the ROC curve from the conventional
report. In a bank
service-desk case study, the predictor improves reassignment forecasts at
first touch, not at incident creation, once the benchmark holds what is
assumed recorded by then.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Code availability}

Code, derived results, the pre-specified protocols, the amendment log and the
verification code are openly available at
\url{https://github.com/sudhirdixit1/cmdb-routing-queue-baseline}, as release
\releaseTag. That release holds the analysis code, the figures and every
result file the paper cites, byte for byte, including those of the
forecast-evaluation checks of Supplement~\ref{app:ijfchecks}; where an
earlier release's generators would print a summary differently, the
supplement's corrections register records it.
The archive contains a \texttt{Dockerfile} and a \texttt{requirements.lock}
pinning every transitive dependency; \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} gives the exact
commands and the expected runtime. \textbf{Bit-exact reproduction is claimed
inside that container --- \imageDigest\ --- and not outside it.} On a different processor architecture, cells whose design carries no
high-cardinality register agree to floating-point tolerance, while cells that
carry one can differ materially in every metric --- the register ties test
rows to equal predictions, and a difference in the last bit unties them, so a
rank-based metric steps. The divergence tracks the register's cardinality and
not the learner: it is present in both model families and absent from the
low-cardinality baselines of each. \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} measures it for both
families and states a tolerance per condition, and
\texttt{scripts/check\_reproduction.py} re-runs part of the surface and fails
if one is exceeded. A reader checking a \emph{digit} therefore needs the
container; a reader checking a \emph{conclusion} does not.
